01Library

It learns your way of tagging.

A neural network listens to every track and suggests tags for set time, style and character. Tag about 40 tracks that SYRO picks for you — from then on it tags your way. It can also read tags from your folder names.

  • Filter by two or three tags at once
  • “Sounds alike” — tracks with a similar sound
  • Duplicate finder
  • Tags written by other apps are kept — SYRO only removes what you removed

FAQ
What does SYRO do?

It tags your DJ library by sound and keeps it in sync with Rekordbox, turns recorded mixes into tracklists, and builds new mixes — with stems, transitions, mastering, a cover and publishing.

Do I need an internet connection?

Once, to download the models during setup. After that SYRO works offline; online recognition and publishing use the internet only when you start them.

Is my music private?

Yes. Your files are analyzed on your Mac and are never uploaded — except a mix you choose to publish.

Does it replace Rekordbox?

No — it works with it. SYRO prepares your library, cues and playlists; you keep playing in Rekordbox.
